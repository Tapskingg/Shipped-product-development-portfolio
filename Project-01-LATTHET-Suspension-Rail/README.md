# LÄTTHET Suspension Rail

## Product

https://www.ikea.com/se/en/p/laetthet-suspension-rail-00386375/

## My Role

Mechanical Engineer – Product Development & Industrialisation

## Engineering Challenge

Develop and industrialise a precision-adjustable load-bearing suspension system capable of supporting ≥140 kg while maintaining repeatable adjustment performance, assembly robustness, and global manufacturability.

## What I Did

- Designed and industrialised a cam-actuated load-bearing adjustment mechanism.
- Defined critical functional requirements including torque limits (0–3 Nm), adjustment accuracy (±2 mm), pull-out requirements, and structural performance targets.
- Engineered interactions between rail, bracket, and fastening interfaces while controlling tolerance stack-up and alignment.
- Led Design FMEA activities to identify failure modes and establish mitigation strategies.
- Defined verification plans combining physical testing, simulation, and documentation.
- Managed industrialisation across European and Chinese suppliers, ensuring material equivalence, process capability alignment, and compliance with chemical and mechanical requirements.
- Eliminated thread-locking constraints that generated excessive assembly torque and reduced user-adjustment effort.

## Results

- 18.4% component cost reduction.
- Reduced unit cost from €0.81 to €0.65.
- €104,365 realised savings on baseline volumes.
- €423,982 projected savings at scale.
- Improved adjustment usability and installation experience.
- Delivered globally manufacturable solution with consistent performance across supplier environments.
