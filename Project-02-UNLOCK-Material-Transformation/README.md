## My Role

Mechanical Engineer – Material Engineering, Validation & Industrialisation

## Engineering Challenge

Replace zinc-plated steel with pre-galvanized steel across global suppliers while maintaining safety performance, durability, manufacturability, assembly compatibility, and regulatory compliance.

## Technical Ownership

- Material selection and specification definition
- Validation strategy and test methodology development
- D-FMEA and technical risk assessment
- Supplier industrialisation and production implementation
- Functional verification and durability testing
- Sustainability and lifecycle analysis

## Validation & Testing

- Climate chamber conditioning: 28°C / 85% RH ↔ 28°C / 25% RH
- 100-cycle unlock fatigue verification
- 200-cycle durability validation
- 400 N pull-out performance verification
- Spring-force and hinge-friction characterization
- Functional recovery verification after conditioning

## Industrialisation

Implemented across European and Chinese suppliers with aligned material specifications, process capability requirements, quality controls, and production validation criteria.

## Results

- 13–21% cost reduction
- Up to 25% longer component life
- Up to 20% lower carbon footprint
- Improved recyclability
- Eliminated electroplating process waste
- Established reusable KPI-driven material selection methodology
